Spotify Widget — one-time setup

This page gets you a refresh token so the OBS widgets can read your currently playing song straight from the Spotify API. Open this page in your normal browser (Chrome/Edge/Firefox), not in OBS. You only do this once.

Step 1 — Create a Spotify app (free)

  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify account.
  2. Click Create app. Name/description can be anything (e.g. "OBS Widget").
  3. Under Redirect URIs add exactly: http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback
  4. Check Web API, save, then copy the app's Client ID.

Step 2 — Authorize

Paste your Client ID and click Authorize. Spotify will ask you to approve, then send you to a page that fails to load (that's expected — nothing is listening on 127.0.0.1).
